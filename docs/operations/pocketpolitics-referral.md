# Candidate-profile referral to PocketPolitics

Reviewed 8 September 2026 by browsing the public [welcome page](https://pocketpolitics.io/welcome), its [open demo](https://pocketpolitics.io/?demo=1), and the site's [source description](https://pocketpolitics.io/juridik/kallor).

PocketPolitics presents elected representatives, political assignments, committees, remuneration and municipal documents. The public demo is a limited, account-free preview for Marks kommun. Full access is offered through a subscription. The welcome page also has an interest form for requesting another municipality. Direct navigation to `#intresseanmalan` did not scroll to the form during verification, so the referral does not promise a direct form shortcut.

Each loaded Politicalverse candidate profile includes one referral card after its historical results and before source disclosures. This is the natural next step from election performance to political work. The main link goes straight to the demo, reducing the steps before a visitor can explore the product; the secondary link goes to the welcome page for features and subscription information. Both identify the separate product and its owner, William Rydh. English copy explains that the destination demo is in Swedish.

Do not label this as a complete profile for the selected candidate. No verified cross-product person mapping or public candidate-specific route was established. The demo's Mark coverage must remain explicit on profiles for other municipalities, regions and parliamentary constituencies. Never manufacture name-only deep links, promise national coverage, or describe subscription features as freely accessible.

Links use fixed UTM tags: `utm_source=politicalverse`, `utm_medium=referral`, `utm_campaign=candidate_profiles`, and `utm_content=sv_demo`, `en_demo`, `sv_overview` or `en_overview`. These distinguish destination and language when the destination's reporting supports UTM attribution. No candidate name, identifier, filters or visitor-specific data are added. This does not install analytics or promise that PocketPolitics currently reports conversion events.

The links open a new tab with an accessible announcement, preserving the election profile and its filters. No PocketPolitics scripts, iframes or assets are loaded on Politicalverse. The card uses scoped CSS, existing theme colors and 44px-or-larger link targets.
