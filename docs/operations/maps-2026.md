# 2026 result maps

The 2026 explorer now uses the official 2026 GeoJSON for all 21 administrative counties, 290 municipalities and 6,312 physical districts. It preserves the historical explorer and its 2022 geometry. Both views offer party, year, geography, share, share-point change and turnout controls. The current explorer additionally keeps RD/RF/KF and counting-stage selection.

`npm run data:import:map:2026` downloads the pinned official national GIS archive and checks every physical identity against the accepted 2026 preparation register. Mapshaper 0.6.113 dissolves administrative boundaries and simplifies with `keep-shapes`. The normalized, compressed output has a checksum manifest and is checked during `data:verify`. Maps are fetched per selected geographic level through static `.json` routes; the shared page bundle does not contain all district polygons.

The existing five-minute live collector additionally verifies each archive's signed district distribution and reconciles district counts, electorate and every displayed party bucket against its municipality and electoral-area total. Districts are compact arrays in canonical `PARTY_IDS` order: eight parliamentary parties plus other votes. Administrative party tables retain their full official party identities. No new live-data publication files, branch merges or unrelated infrastructure changes are required.

National RD revision 860's district JSON is 204,169,442 bytes because the authority also includes ballot-list personal votes. The decompression bound is 256 MiB only for the national production final-count district file; other bounds, checksums, RSA signature and certificate checks remain intact. Collector concurrency is two to limit peak memory. Archive members can have different generation timestamps; the signed archive checksum and source revision must match, and each file's timestamp is independently checked.

Collection rows have no polygon or electorate. They appear once in the district list and higher-level totals; turnout is unavailable for those rows. 2022 comparisons come from the same signed 2026 archive and only its declared comparable districts, not a same-code join. Changed boundaries and missing party baselines remain missing. Historical RD administrative trends retain 2010–2018 and use the archive's 2022 baseline alongside 2026. RF/KF comparisons show the years available from their signed source, without borrowing RD history.

A map-source failure cannot discard independently validated current area totals. The new area remains available, its district rows are withheld rather than mixed with an older generation, and a source failure is reported for retry. Normal archive failures retain the last validated area. Local development reads the accepted checked-in area snapshot for reproducible UI checks; production continues to poll the shared feed and only falls back to the static snapshot on initial connection failure.
